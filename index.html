How It Works

Follow these simple steps to use our iPhone checking services.

1

Enter Your IMEI

Enter your device's 15-digit IMEI number in the checking tool. Make sure the number is correct before continuing.

2

Select Your Check Type

Choose the available checking service: Basic Check, iCloud / FMI, Carrier Check, Blacklist GSMA or Full PRO Report.

3

Submit Your Request

Submit your request and wait for the checking process or manual verification, depending on the service.

4

Receive Your Report

Review your available device information and receive your report when the service is completed.

Important Notice

IMEI reports depend on the available data sources. Results may vary depending on the device and service. Always verify important information before purchasing a device.
